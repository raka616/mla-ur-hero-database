Featured Mechanics: Genesis Shar

Gameplay observations supplied by the primary data contributor.

Clone mechanic

Copy an enemy hero

Genesis Shar can copy an enemy hero as an ally, adding another unit to the team. This may be useful when the copied hero is especially powerful and can be turned against its own team.

The contributor notes that the clone can use skills, not only basic attacks.

Upgrade interaction

Skill and Orlay effects

At full Awaken (A0), the clone is described as inheriting effects from Genesis Shar’s own Soul Skill and Orlay Skill levels, rather than the copied hero’s own upgrade levels.

Stats scaling

HP and Attack

According to the contributor’s observation, clone HP and Attack scale with Genesis Shar’s Star Level. The clone may reach 140% of the enemy’s corresponding stats, subject to a cap of 700% of Genesis Shar’s base stats.

Star Level 9

Realm Effect and cap

The contributor notes that Star Level 9 unlocks Realm Effect Lv. 4. Improving Soul Vessel, Orlay, and Glory can raise Genesis Shar’s stats and therefore the clone’s cap, according to this observation.

Contributor-provided formula at Star Level 9:

Clone stat = min(140% × enemy stat, 700% × Genesis Shar base stat)

If 140% of the enemy’s stat exceeds the 700% cap based on Genesis Shar’s base stat, the clone is limited by that cap. This formula is recorded as a contributor observation and has not been independently verified here.
