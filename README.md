Featured Mechanics: Genesis Shar

The following mechanics are documented from contributor-provided gameplay observations and should be verified against in-game descriptions and testing.

- Genesis Shar can create a copy of an enemy hero as an allied unit.

- The copied unit can influence the battle through its available actions and crowd-control interactions.

- At full Awaken (A0), the copy is described as inheriting effects tied to Genesis Shar's Soul Skill and Orlay Skill levels.

- The copy's HP and Attack are described as scaling with Genesis Shar's Star Level.

- Contributor-provided maximum scaling model at Star Level 9:
  
  "Clone stats = min(140% × enemy stats, 700% × Genesis Shar stats)"

This formula is recorded as a contributor-provided model and requires in-game verification before being treated as an authoritative game formula.
